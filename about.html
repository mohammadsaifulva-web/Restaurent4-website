Our Beginnings

From a Dream
to Excellence

Saif Dining was born in 2010 from a simple yet powerful vision: to bring the world's finest culinary traditions to Riyadh and redefine what fine dining means in Saudi Arabia.

Our founder, Saiful Islam, spent years training under Michelin-starred chefs in Paris, Tokyo and New York before returning to create something truly extraordinary here at home.

Today, with 3 Michelin stars and 15 years of serving the region's most discerning guests, we continue to push the boundaries of gastronomy every single day.
