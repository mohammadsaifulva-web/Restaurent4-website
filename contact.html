Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

+

Yes! We have a dedicated vegetarian menu and can accommodate vegan requests with 24 hours notice. Please inform our team when making your reservation.

Is there a dress code at Saif Dining?

+

We request smart casual to formal attire. Shorts, sportswear and flip-flops are not permitted. We want every guest to feel the elegance of the dining experience.

How far in advance should I book?

+

We recommend booking at least 3-7 days in advance for weekend dining and 2-4 weeks for private events. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability.

Do you cater for large groups?

+

Absolutely! Our private dining room accommodates up to 20 guests, and we can arrange exclusive buyouts of the full restaurant for groups of 50+. Contact us directly.
